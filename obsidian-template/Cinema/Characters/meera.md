# 👤 Character Dossier: Dr. Meera Sen

- **Created:** 2026-10-23
- **Associated Project:** [[Cinema/Projects/project_mirage/Overview|Project Mirage]]
- **Profiled By:** Kino Cinema Subagent

---

### 1. Archetype & Story Role
- **Archetype:** The Catalyst / Moral Compass
- **Role:** Foil & Whistleblower

### 2. External Want vs Internal Need
- **External Want:** To retrieve the neurological suppression trial data and expose rogue pharmaceutical research.
- **Internal Need:** To recognize that empirical data cannot heal the grief of losing her brother.

### 3. The Ghost (Past Trauma)
- Her investigative journalist brother disappeared after tracing black-budget memory modification research to senior ministry officials.

### 4. Fatal Flaw (Hamartia)
- **Clinical Detachment:** Tends to treat dangerous real-world crises like laboratory experiments until violence directly touches her.

### 5. Speech Rhythm & Mannerisms
- Rapid-fire, razor-sharp medical precision. Cuts through tactical military jargon with cold scientific facts.

### 6. Visual Motif
- Wireframe titanium spectacles; black waterproof parka; vintage audio recorder hanging from neck strap.
