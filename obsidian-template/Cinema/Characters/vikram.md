# 👤 Character Dossier: ACP Vikram "Ghost"

- **Created:** 2026-10-23
- **Associated Project:** [[Cinema/Projects/project_mirage/Overview|Project Mirage]]
- **Profiled By:** Kino Cinema Subagent

---

### 1. Archetype & Story Role
- **Archetype:** The Tragic Anti-Hero / Reluctant Executioner
- **Role:** Protagonist

### 2. External Want vs Internal Need
- **External Want:** To piece together the last 72 hours, uncover the intelligence mole, and clear his name.
- **Internal Need:** To confront the moral cost of his past violence and accept that he cannot serve both state law and human justice.

### 3. The Ghost (Past Trauma)
- Three years ago, Vikram authorized an unsanctioned tactical raid on a suspected terror safehouse. The intel was flawed; an innocent civilian family died in the crossfire. His survivor's guilt made him emotionally numb and reckless.

### 4. Fatal Flaw (Hamartia)
- **Pathological Solitude:** Believes asking for help gets people killed. He cuts off allies at the exact moment collaborative trust is mandatory.

### 5. Speech Rhythm & Mannerisms
- Monosyllabic, cold, hyper-observant. Never engages in small talk. Under extreme physical danger or interrogation, his voice drops to a calm, raspy whisper.

### 6. Visual Motif & Physicality
- Heavy dark charcoal trenchcoat soaked with sea spray; blackened knuckles; constantly fidgets with empty bullet casings in his left coat pocket.
