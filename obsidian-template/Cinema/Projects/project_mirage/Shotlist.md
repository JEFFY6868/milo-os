# 🎥 Director's Shot Division: Project Mirage (Dhuruva)

### Scene 01: Pier Ambush & Awakening (Night / Rain)
- **Shot 01 [Extreme Wide - 24mm Anamorphic]:** High crane descent from dark thunderclouds to submerged dock pilings. Single sodium floodlight casts amber silhouette on seawater.
- **Shot 02 [Medium Steadicam - 35mm Prime]:** Gliding behind Vikram's shoulder as he drags himself onto the wooden dock. Cold cyan rim lighting on soaked jacket.
- **Shot 03 [Extreme Close-up - 85mm T1.4]:** Macro focus on dog tags clutched in Vikram's trembling hand. Seawater and blood dissolve together.
- **Shot 04 [Dutch Low Angle - 40mm]:** Handheld micro-tremors from water level looking up at the empty pistol slide locked back against the stormy sky.

### Scene 04: Derelict Dock Warehouse Confrontation
- **Shot 01 [Master Two-Shot - 35mm]:** Vikram and Rudra framed at opposite ends of waterlogged floor. Steam vents create depth layers.
- **Shot 02 [Slow Creep Push - 50mm]:** Push-in on Rudra's silver cigarette case flicking open. Match strike audio sharp cut.
- **Shot 03 [Extreme Low Angle - 28mm]:** Vikram refuses to look up as red laser diode glides across his chest.
