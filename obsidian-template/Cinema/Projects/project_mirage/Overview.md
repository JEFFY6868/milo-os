# 🎬 Film Project: Project Mirage (Dhuruva)

- **Created:** 2026-10-23
- **Director / Writer:** Jeffy
- **Co-Pilot:** Kino Cinema Subagent
- **Status:** In Development (Screenplay Draft 1)

---

## 📌 High-Concept Logline
> An undercover intelligence operative wakes up half-submerged under a bombed Mumbai dock with total short-term amnesia, forced to reconstruct his last 72 hours before the rogue syndicate he infiltrated triggers a synchronized city blackout—only to discover that he himself orchestrated the syndicate's master plan before losing his memory.

---

## 🎭 Genre & Tone Architecture
- **Primary Genre:** Neo-Noir Psychological Thriller / Action Espionage
- **Cinema Comps:** *Memories of Murder*, *Vikram Vedha*, *The Bourne Identity*, *Prisoners*
- **Visual Tone:** Heavy rain, chiaroscuro shadow play, sodium-vapor amber street lamps, desaturated coastal blues, high-contrast framing.
- **Audio Atmosphere:** Low-frequency sub-bass drones, isolated wet footsteps, muted gunfire foley, atmospheric cello motifs.

---

## ⚖️ Central Moral Argument (Theme)
> **Justice vs Law:** Can a man hold onto his morality when the system he swore to protect operates on sanctioned deceit? In a corrupt world, does erasing one's identity make one innocent or an accomplice?
