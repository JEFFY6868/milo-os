# 📜 Screenplay Beat Sheet: Project Mirage (Dhuruva)

Director's structured 3-Act / 9-Sequence Screenplay Beat Sheet.

---

## 🎭 ACT 1: Setup & Inciting Incident (00 — 30 Min)

### Scene 01 • EXT. DOCK PIER - NIGHT [Tension: 8/10]
- **The Rain Ambush:** Vikram wakes up gasping under a submerged shipping container. Saltwater stings a severe head wound. His 9mm Glock is locked back on empty. Around his wrist hang the dog tags of a fallen officer. Amnesia is total.
- *Visual:* Extreme wide crane down through storm rain; silhouette against harbor floodlight.

### Scene 02 • INT. DERELICT SAFE HOUSE - NIGHT [Tension: 6.5/10]
- **The Burner Phone Signal:** A SIM-less phone rings with an encrypted priority alert from Chief Menon declaring Vikram compromised and dispatching a liquidation team.
- *Visual:* Harsh overhead bulb swinging; water dripping into a galvanized metal bucket.

### Scene 03 • EXT. RAILWAY FOOTBRIDGE - MIDNIGHT [Tension: 9/10]
- **Crossing the Threshold:** Vikram burns his intelligence credentials and lets the ash drop onto the electrified tracks. He resolves to hunt both the agency mole and the syndicate. Point of no return.

---

## ⚡ ACT 2: Conflict & Midpoint Turn (30 — 85 Min)

### Scene 04 • INT. METRO TUNNEL - NIGHT [Tension: 7.5/10]
- **The Shadow Interrogation:** Vikram traps Rudra inside an idling maintenance train. Rudra reveals that Vikram wasn't just investigating the syndicate—he planned their escape route.
- *Subtext:* Rudra offers Vikram a cigarette using his silver lighter; Vikram recognizes the engraved monogram.

### Scene 05 • INT. MUMBAI CITY HALL - DAY [Tension: 9.5/10]
- **The Midpoint Blackout:** Vikram secures the encrypted server drive during a diplomatic gala, but the power cuts out. When the strobe kicks in, the armed squad salutes him as their commander.

### Scene 06 • EXT. DOCK CRANE CABIN - DAWN [Tension: 8.5/10]
- **Dark Night of the Soul:** Wounded and isolated high above the harbor. Dr. Meera's comm channel cuts out. Vikram discovers medical vials showing his amnesia was intentionally self-inflicted to withstand interrogation.

---

## 💥 ACT 3: Climax & Moral Resolution (85 — 120 Min)

### Scene 07 • INT. OLD SOUND ARCHIVE - NIGHT [Tension: 8.8/10]
- **The Trigger Audio Tape:** Vikram plays back an analog micro-cassette he hid inside his service firearm before the memory wipe. His past self commands his final tactical operation.

### Scene 08 • EXT. DOCK 14 WAREHOUSE - NIGHT [Tension: 10/10]
- **The Grand Climax Duel:** Torrential rain. Vikram confronts Chief Menon and Rudra simultaneously under swinging cargo cranes. Philosophical confrontation over state corruption ends in a deadly sacrificial gambit.

### Scene 09 • EXT. MUMBAI HARBOR - DAWN [Tension: 4/10]
- **Final Image & Resolution:** Golden dawn breaks over the Arabian Sea. Vikram drops the remaining drive into the ocean and walks into the bustling harbor crowd, nameless and untraceable.
